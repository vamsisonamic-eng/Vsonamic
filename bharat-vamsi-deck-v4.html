The Risk Equation · Structural Continuity · dentsu India

The risk isn't a person leaving.
It's an operational bottleneck.

As pipeline velocity accelerates, capability and demand diverge — and one person is the bridge holding it secure.

The Bottleneck Risk
Single-threaded operational ownership.
Pacing, optimization, QA and escalation across 5 platforms currently route through one person. As campaign velocity climbs toward ~1000/yr, that concentration becomes the account's primary delivery risk — not a personnel risk.
The Capability Gap
Pipeline velocity > current staffing.
Volume has grown ~7× with no added headcount. The forward pipeline needs a defined leadership layer to absorb it — without one, expedites and defects re-enter the system the discipline removed.
The Structural Bridge
Formalize the leadership layer.
Promoting and resourcing this role converts a single point of dependency into a documented, repeatable capability — already proven by upskilling 1 AD, 3 Managers & 1 SM. Leadership is the de-risking instrument.
