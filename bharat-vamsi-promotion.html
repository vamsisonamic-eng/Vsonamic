Bharat → NSM activation team
Microsoft Teams · Sep 2025
"All rejected creatives point to URLs under /shopping/. In robots.txt, /shopping/ is disallowed — so DV360's ad crawler can't reach those pages, even though Googlebot isn't blocked. That's the repeated 'Destination not crawlable' rejection. Fix: allow AdsBot/DV360 to crawl /shopping/, or serve alternate crawlable landing URLs."
G
Freddy Luong — Google
Email · reply to the escalation
"You are correct in your assessment regarding some of the blocked paths in the website — that is causing creatives to be flagged and blocked, since Google cannot crawl the page."
Control · DMAIC Outcome Root cause analysis result entered the team's pre-launch QA SOP. Creative-destination crawlability is now a standard trafficking checklist item — failure mode permanently designed out.
