What This Correction Protects

A small correction protects
a very large asset.

1
Resource
Consolidated Platform Ownership
Currently, Bharat manages SSP (DV360, Trade Desk), DSP, Meta, and Pinterest as a single consolidated owner. Transitioning to individual platform owners — the standard model — would require separate specialists for each, each with their own onboarding period and no single owner holding the full pacing picture.
6mo
Transfer Timeline
Knowledge Transfer Risk Window
Reaching full operational competency across all five platforms at $5.3M/week takes a minimum of 6 months. During that window, maintaining the current >98% DIF standard — which the client has formally acknowledged — would require careful oversight and carries delivery risk on a $275M engagement.
2
ADs on Record
Client Relationship Continuity
Both Associate Directors on the account — Raymond Hall and Kyle Lower — have formally credited Bharat by name. The client has specifically recommended the promotion in writing. Continuity of this relationship is most effectively protected by recognising and retaining the individual they have named.
A market-fair correction of ₹15 LPA — structured with most of the increase tied to the delivery targets already being met — is the most efficient way to protect account continuity, client trust, and operational stability on a $275M engagement.
